Privacy Policy for QuickAction

Last updated: 2026-11-02

QuickAction is a desktop application for Windows. We respect your privacy and keep data collection minimal.

Information We Collect

QuickAction does not collect personal information, does not require account sign-in, and does not send usage analytics or telemetry to external servers.

Local Data Storage

QuickAction stores app configuration locally on your device (for example pinned shortcuts and related settings). This data stays on your machine unless you explicitly export or share it.

Network Communication

QuickAction does not run a cloud backend and does not perform background data synchronization.

Third-Party Services

QuickAction itself does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

QuickAction is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when product behavior changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For questions, use the repository issues page: https://github.com/EvilArtist/QuickAction/issues
