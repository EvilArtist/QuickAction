QuickAction

QuickAction is a lightweight Windows dock utility focused on fast access to pinned apps and installed applications.

Available in English and Vietnamese. Choose your language in Settings → Dock & system. Dock settings apply and save automatically.

Privacy

QuickAction does not use telemetry or cloud sync. Read the full policy here: Privacy Policy.
